## Introduction to Doomvibing

- Over the last couple of weeks, I've seen more and more of my friends adopt AI. Some, like me, have had a history in computer science and use it to augment our existing coding, and some others have completely different backgrounds in finance, law, etc. etc.

- An interesting phenomena that (I think) I might be the first to identify is the rise of doomvibing, a combination of doomscrolling and vibecoding. Let me explain.

## Case Study: ASX Terminal

- One of my friends who had no computer science background or experience as of approximately two weeks ago downloaded Claude Code, and from there, mainly on their own (with a little bit of help to identify a) what the terminal is and b) how to write commands in it), wrote a full stack web application with email sign in, API integration, backend and frontend as well as a database.

- Looking at the site, I assessed that I realistically would have taken easily 6-12 months to create something like this before the rise of AI, as the frontend was quite complex and it was not my area of specialty, having majored in Computer Science and not Software Engineering/UI/UX. I was pretty shocked at how fast AI had made this, along with the fact that almost 0 technical knowledge was required to build a project of this complexity.

- Funnily enough, after a call to this friend, I found out that he had lost interest in the project shortly after creating it and had vibecoded another 2-3 full stack web applications, in roughly the same timeline. He joked that he didn't have a long enough attention span to polish it off and ship it, so instead he just kept switching applications when bored.
    - Sidenote, he hit weekly usage limits on Claude Code in under 48 hours.

## Reflections on Doomvibing

- Watching someone build an application that would have taken me months of work in one day, followed by trashing the project because it was no longer intellectually stimulating enough was a unique experience that I honestly did not think I'd ever see in my lifetime. 

- It seems that the same decrease in attention span that has made Shorts/Reels/etc. etc. a preferred form of media consumption over longer videos has now somehow also made it to coding full stack web applications??

- I can't say I've reached definite conclusions or reached some form of enlightenment following this observation. But I can say it is definitely food for thought.

- For anyone who thinks that AI still creates useless, buggy code, maybe you are right, but having witnessed the exponential increase in capability firsthand (and also watching doomvibing become a thing), I would posit the question: 

    - Given where AI was 12 months ago and where it is now, instead of considering what AI's capabilities are now (which are quite frankly, extremely impressive), where do you think AI will be at in 12 months time, and are you choosing to bury your head in the sand or embrace (or at the very least, acknowledge) the change that is coming?

## What's next

- Coding Beacon for so long has perhaps led to me suffering from a mild case of yearning for doomvibing as well. Next week, I'm going to try and crank out multiple small projects I have been meaning to do for a long time on very short timelines and see if I can achieve a state of enlightenment by doomvibing.
