## What I got up to

- Today, rolling out more bug fixes on Beacon was my priority. A bunch of UI bugs were fixed, which leaves the big ticket items before deployment. (Optimistic UI, selective beacons).

- One of my favourite features that I implemented was the international features - being able to see people in different timezones. We will see what the wider public thinks of this when we get to the launch (and I get to figure international deployments).
